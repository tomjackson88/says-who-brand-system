# Says Who? — Brand System

Visual system extracted from the official Says Who? Brand Kit (May 2024).

## Brand

- **Name**: Says Who?
- **Format**: Podcast
- **Host**: Brogan
- **Tagline**: *Intentional Living. Timeless Design.*

## Origin

*Getting There* was one of the fastest-growing podcasts in the UK. At the height of its success, Brogan found herself in deep internal conflict. Conversation after conversation was revealing the same uncomfortable truth: **we are quietly losing what it means to be human.**

So she did something her audience could never have anticipated — she stopped. Not because she ran out of things to say, but because the question changed.

It was no longer *"How do I get there?"*
It became: ***"Who decided where* there *was in the first place?"***

*Says Who?* is the podcast born from that question.

## Positioning

A podcast that interrogates the scripts we inherit — about success, ambition, identity, and what it means to live a human life. Less about reaching somewhere, more about questioning the destination itself.

## Personality

Warm · Honest · Timeless · Intentional · Elevated

Reflective, not reactive. Considered, not contrarian. The brand holds space for doubt and slowness in a culture addicted to certainty and speed.

## Voice

Conversational, reflective, quietly confident. Short declarative statements that invite pause ("Make space for what matters", "You are made of magic"). The host speaks *with* the listener, never *at* them.

## Brand Values

1. **Intentional** — Every conversation has a purpose.
2. **Timeless** — We value depth over trend cycles.
3. **Authentic** — We stay true to the questions that matter.

## Philosophy

We believe that beauty lives in the details, and that thoughtful questions create more meaningful lives than confident answers.

## Color System

| Token | Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--color-bg-deep` | Rich Plum | `#1C0C11` | Primary dark background, dramatic sections |
| `--color-brand` | Merlot | `#3B1F24` | Primary brand color, CTA surfaces, cards |
| `--color-accent` | Burnt Oak | `#8A5B3A` | Warm accent, icons, highlights |
| `--color-neutral-warm` | Warm Sand | `#B9A998` | Mid-tone surfaces, borders |
| `--color-surface` | Chalk | `#EBE6DF` | Default light background (verify against source — kit shows `#EB66DF` but swatch reads cream) |
| `--color-secondary` | Olive | `#4F5A2D` | Secondary accent, grounding sections |

**Palette character**: A grounded, warm palette that evokes sophistication, calm and timeless elegance.

**Usage rules**
- Light mode: Chalk surface + Rich Plum text + Merlot/Burnt Oak accents
- Dark mode: Rich Plum surface + Chalk text + Warm Sand secondary text
- Olive and Burnt Oak are accents — never dominant
- Never pair Merlot text on Rich Plum (insufficient contrast)

## Typography

**Primary — Canela** (high-contrast serif, editorial)
- Display / Headline — Canela Regular
- Accent / Pull quote — Canela Italic

**Secondary — Suisse Int'l** (neo-grotesque sans)
- Subhead — Suisse Int'l Medium
- Body — Suisse Int'l Regular
- Micro / Labels — Suisse Int'l Regular, uppercase, tracked +0.08–0.12em

**Scale**

| Role | Size / Line-height | Font |
|---|---|---|
| Display | 64 / 1.1 | Canela Regular |
| H1 | 48 / 1.15 | Canela Regular |
| H2 | 32 / 1.2 | Canela Regular |
| Quote | 24 / 1.4 | Canela Italic |
| Subhead | 18 / 1.4 | Suisse Int'l Medium |
| Body | 16 / 1.6 | Suisse Int'l Regular |
| Label | 12 / 1.3 | Suisse Int'l Regular, uppercase, +0.12em |

## Logo

- **Primary logo**: "Says Who?" wordmark in Canela
- **Secondary logo**: "SAYS WHO?" in Suisse Int'l, uppercase, tracked
- **Logo mark**: "SW?" lockup in a square (dark-on-light or reversed)
- **Submark**: "SW?" inside a thin circle

**Rule**: Always ensure legibility and contrast across applications.

---

## Spacing & Layout

- 8pt base rhythm: `4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64 / 96`
- Generous negative space — whitespace is a brand asset, not a gap
- Modular grid with clear column/section breaks
- Editorial numbering (`01`, `02`, …) as section markers in Suisse small caps

---

## Iconography

- Thin stroke (1.25–1.5px), rounded geometric line icons
- Monoline style, never filled, never mixed with emoji
- Icon size tokens: `16 / 20 / 24`
- Always paired with a short label (conversational tone)

---

## Art Direction

**Mood**: Warm · Honest · Timeless · Intentional · Elevated

**Guidelines**
- Use warm, natural lighting (golden hour, soft shadows)
- Focus on texture and detail (linen, ceramic, wood, stone, drapery)
- Keep compositions calm and intentional
- Use rich neutrals with purpose
- Text should feel like a conversation

**Subjects**: Interior/lifestyle, quiet and composed. Single focal point, generous negative space.

---

## Motion

- Slow, deliberate: 300–500ms for sections, 200ms for micro-interactions
- Easing: ease-out, gentle — no bounce, no spring overshoot
- Fades and subtle reveals only; avoid flashy transforms

---

## Voice & Copy Examples

- "A life of intention creates a legacy of meaning."
- "Make space for what matters."
- "You are made of magic."
- "This is a sign that a major shift is about to happen in your life."
- "re : set · re : align · re : start · re : claim · re : ignite"

**Tone rules**
- Short, declarative, reflective
- Lowercase for intimacy; small caps for structure
- No urgency, no exclamation marks, no growth-hack voice

---

## The Says Who? Experience

Four pillars of every episode:
1. **Curated Conversations** — Guests chosen for depth, not reach.
2. **Thoughtful Framing** — Questions designed to unsettle, not confirm.
3. **Meaningful Impact** — Listeners leave changed, not just informed.
4. **Lasting Relationships** — A community built on honesty over hype.

---

## Anti-patterns

- ❌ Bright, saturated, or neon colors
- ❌ Geometric sans-serif display (replaces Canela's editorial character)
- ❌ Emoji icons, filled glyphs, multi-color illustrations
- ❌ High-density dashboards or aggressive grids
- ❌ Photography with cool tones, flat lighting, or busy subjects
- ❌ Exclamation marks, urgency copy, growth-hack voice
